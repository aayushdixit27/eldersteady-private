# ElderSteady Private — the argument

California will not let a facility put a camera in her room; at home the same question is yours — this is a fall alert that answers it in bytes.

## The claim in three lines

Numbers before the fall: posture, sit-to-stand, floor time, rhythm, and company are computed on the MLA from keypoints. **Provenance: implemented; posture values measured in one controlled one-person test; rules guessed.**
Fall alert: three real falls on camera and one controlled posture-test fall emitted events on the Modalix board. **Provenance: measured.**
Bytes-out counter: `1 : 303` at frame `150` and `1 : 98` over `13,350` frames on `end0`. **Provenance: measured NIC bytes; ratios computed.**

the counter proves the board leaked nothing; in the demo the camera is the Mac.

## Objections

### SiMa silicon engineer

Where are camera-to-alert latency, total compute, power, and proof of what the outgoing bytes contain?

The measured `8.1–8.3 ms` is MLA inference only; the counter measures all `end0` traffic (computed `1 : 303` at measured frame `150`, computed `1 : 98` over `13,350` measured frames) and proves neither end-to-end latency nor payload contents.

### Daughter who just bought a Kami camera

What did you prove that my Kami cannot do, and where is the alert I receive across town?

The phone page is local on the Mac hotspot, no relay across town exists yet, and measured posture test 4 is the only controlled one-person pass.

### Privacy lawyer familiar with PIN 15-RM-01

Why would this ledger satisfy a room-camera waiver without consent, access, and shutoff procedures?

The ledger supports a review; it does not replace a waiver, consent, or a shutoff.

### The other fall-detection team

Where are the held-out homes, sensitivity, false alarms, and proof that these measures predict falls?

There are no held-out homes, sensitivity, or false-alarm numbers; measured evidence is one controlled posture test and three real falls on camera.

## What we will not say

- No “first ever” or universal novelty claim.
- No prediction or warning-before-a-fall claim.
- No cross-town alert-delivery claim.
- No clinical accuracy, sensitivity, or false-alarm claim.
- No “no images in the payload” claim beyond what the counter shows.

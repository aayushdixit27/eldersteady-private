# ElderSteady Private — Silver Bundle, SiMa track, AI Infra Summit Hackathon

*Announced 18 September 2026 (lablab.ai, 9:10 AM). Recorded by the setup lane the same day.*

## The result

| Place | Team | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | HomeAngelAI | $2,000 — DevKit + webcam + Maker Robot Arm Kit |
| **Silver** | **ElderSteady Private** | **$1,600 — Modalix MLSoC DevKit + webcam** |
| Bronze | PushVAI | $1,500 — Modalix MLSoC DevKit |

Track brief: "Build Physical AI that sees, understands, and acts — at the edge, on Modalix." $5,000 in hardware across the three bundles; every bundle built around the DevKit the teams worked on (50 TOPS at sub-10 W, 8 Arm Cortex-A65 cores, 16 GB LPDDR5, 500 GB NVMe). Another team on the track also built fall detection; we differentiated on the ledger.

## What the judges could check

- 8.1–8.3 ms per frame, YOLO26-m pose INT8 on the MLA, board-local on NVMe, NFS unmounted (measured)
- 3.36 MB of video in, 11 KB out on end0 over 150 frames — 1:303 (measured; long-run 1:98 and why)
- `cat /sys/class/net/end0/statistics/tx_bytes` over serial matches the screen
- Real falls on camera by lean and by floor-lie; sit-to-stand counted; 11 captured logs in evidence/captures
- Every number labelled measured / computed / fixture / guessed

## The build in numbers

Two days. One founder. Six agents at peak: an architect session, this setup session, three Claude Code lanes, one GPT-6 research lane, and ~25 Codex build runs (~2.3 M tokens, ≈ $7 of the $200 key). Four controlled on-camera posture tests; five classifier designs, four retired. Three crucible rounds; one idea cleared the 9.5 gate (pixels-in / bytes-out, 9.6). 22 documents in the Senso folder; CURRENT.md revised six times.

## What the win means for the roadmap

The prize is the DevKit plus a webcam: the first roadmap line — the camera on the box, so the honest line "the demo camera is the Mac" goes away — is now hardware we own. Next: audio on the MLA, the det+pose second model run live, calibration as an install step, one real family alert, and the facility-waiver conversation with the ledger as evidence.
